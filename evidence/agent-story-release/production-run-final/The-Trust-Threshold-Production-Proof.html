Slide 1 of 7: Scene 1 — The Plausibility Trap

ORIENT

Plausible is not production. The threshold line begins here.

Every leader here has watched an AI generate a deck that looked right — until someone tried to edit, export, or defend it. NodeSlide's promise is narrower and harder: agents that finish coherent, editable, published decks. The threshold line sits at 24% intensity: oriented, not yet convinced.

  • Tension: impressive drafts, unverifiable outcomes
  • Promise: coherence, editability, and publication as inspectable artifacts
